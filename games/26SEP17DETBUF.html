    Whole numbers vs. the hook

    "Whole numbers only"

    The instinct is real: losing "by the hook" — the favourite wins by exactly 4 when you had −4.5 — feels like theft, and a whole-number line at −4 would have refunded you.

    But look at the ladder. Kalshi has no whole-number lines. Every market is "by over 4.5", which is the same contract as "by 5 or more". There is nothing to push; the hook is built into every line on the exchange. "Whole numbers only" on Kalshi means moneyline only.

    "That's dumb"

    Right conclusion, wrong reason. Whether a line reads 4.5 or "5+" is wording. What matters is the price of the margin you are giving up: the chart above shows how much probability sits on exactly 3 and exactly 7. A half point through a key number is expensive; a half point through 5 is nearly free.

    So the rule that actually helps: compare the price to the probability, not the line to your superstition — which is exactly what the System does.
